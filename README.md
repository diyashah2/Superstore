# Super Store Analysis Dashboard
- This project is a Power BI dashboard for analyzing Superstore sales data. It offers a visual overview of sales trends, category performance, and customer behavior through interactive reports.

  -  Key Features:
      -  Sales Performance Overview: Track sales, profit, and trends by segment, region, and time.
      -  Category & Product Analysis: Understand top-performing categories and sub-categories.
      -  Customer Insights: Analyze customer segments, buying patterns, and retention opportunities.
      -  Interactive Filters: Drill down by year, segment, region, and more.

  -  Business Insights:
      -  Highest Sales: Consumer segment and Technology category dominate.
      -  Top Region: West leads in performance; South & Central need improvement.
      -  Strategy: Seasonal campaigns, bundle offers, and customer targeting boost growth.

  -  Tools Used:
      -  Power BI
      -  Python(Data Cleaning)

Ideal for retail analysts and business teams to make data-driven decisions quickly and effectively.
